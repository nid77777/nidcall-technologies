# ApexCall Technologies

**ApexCall Technologies** is a registered business communications platform that provides dedicated second phone numbers for businesses and individuals. Users receive a separate number to make calls and send messages without exposing their personal number.

## Use Case

ApexCall enables end users to:

- Obtain a dedicated local, toll-free, or international phone number
- Send and receive messages and voice calls through that number
- Use the number for business communication, personal privacy, marketplace transactions, or travel

All messaging initiated through ApexCall is **person-to-person (P2P)** or **application-to-person (A2P)** with explicit user consent. We do not support mass marketing campaigns, political messaging, or unsolicited outreach.

---

## Opt-In

Users provide consent through one of the following methods:

1. **Web sign-up** — the user creates an account at [https://apexcall.net](https://apexcall.net), agrees to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and explicitly selects a number to activate
2. **In-app action** — the user initiates a call or SMS from within the ApexCall app; no message is sent without a deliberate user action
3. **Keyword opt-in** — where applicable, users text a keyword to a number to subscribe to a specific notification type

Consent records are stored per user and are available for audit upon request.

---

## Opt-Out

- Recipients may reply **STOP** to any message to immediately unsubscribe
- Reply **HELP** to receive support contact information
- Opt-out requests are processed within seconds and no further messages are sent
- Users can also manage subscriptions directly at [https://apexcall.net](https://apexcall.net)

---

## Message Types

Messages sent through ApexCall fall into the following categories:

- **Transactional** — account alerts, call/SMS notifications, voicemail notifications
- **Conversational** — user-to-user or user-to-contact exchanges via their dedicated number
- **Verification** — one-time passcodes sent to the user's registered contact

Message frequency varies by user activity. Standard message and data rates may apply.

---

## Prohibited Uses

The following are strictly prohibited on the ApexCall platform:

- Spam, phishing, or unsolicited mass messaging
- Robocalling or autodialedmarketing without prior written consent
- Harassment, threats, or hate speech
- Impersonation or caller ID spoofing
- Any content involving illegal activity, CSAM, or controlled substances

Violations result in immediate account suspension and are reported to relevant authorities where required.
