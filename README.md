# ApexCall Technologies

**ApexCall Technologies** provides virtual phone numbers for businesses and individuals — enabling private, professional communication without exposing your real number.

## What We Offer

- **Virtual phone numbers** — local, toll-free, and international numbers in minutes
- **SMS & Voice** — send and receive calls and texts from any device
- **Business workspace** — shared inbox, call logs, voicemail, and contact management
- **Privacy-first** — keep your personal number private for dating, marketplace selling, travel, and more
- **Affordable credits** — pay only for what you use, no monthly commitment required
